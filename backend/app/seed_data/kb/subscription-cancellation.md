# Subscription Cancellation & Downgrades

- Customers can cancel anytime from Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription; access continues until the end of the current paid period.
- Cancellation does not trigger an automatic refund for the current period unless requested within 14 days of the most recent charge.
- Downgrading from annual to monthly takes effect at the next renewal date, not immediately.
- If a customer cites a specific missing feature or bug as their cancellation reason, note it and offer relevant fixes/workarounds before processing the cancellation.
