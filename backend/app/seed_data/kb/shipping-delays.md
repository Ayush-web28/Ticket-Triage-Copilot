# Shipping Delays & Tracking

- Standard shipping is 5-7 business days; expedited is 2-3 business days.
- Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of order fulfillment. If a customer hasn't received one after 48 hours, check the fulfillment queue for a stuck order.
- Packages showing "in transit" for more than 10 business days with no movement should be treated as lost -- offer a free reshipment or full refund, customer's choice.
- Customs delays on international orders are outside our control; provide the carrier's customs contact info and offer a partial shipping-cost refund as goodwill.
