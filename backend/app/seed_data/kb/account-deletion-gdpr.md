# Account Deletion & Data Requests (GDPR/CCPA)

- Customers can request full account deletion from Settings > Privacy > Delete Account, or by emailing privacy@company.example.
- Deletion requests must be fulfilled within 30 days per GDPR Article 17. Confirm identity via the email on file before processing.
- Data export requests (a copy of all personal data) are fulfilled within 30 days as a downloadable JSON archive.
- Deletion is irreversible after the 14-day grace period; inform customers clearly before confirming.
- Billing records may be retained for up to 7 years for tax/legal compliance even after account deletion -- disclose this to the customer.
