# Security Incidents & Unauthorized Access

- Any report that an account was hacked, compromised, or accessed without permission -- including logins from an unfamiliar location, unrecognized device, or country the customer has never visited -- must be escalated to the Security team immediately. Do not attempt to resolve via a standard reply.
- Immediately advise the customer to change their password and revoke active sessions from Settings > Security > Active Sessions.
- Do not confirm or deny specific security details in writing until the Security team has investigated.
- Log the report time, affected account, login location, and reported symptoms for the incident record.
